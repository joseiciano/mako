
# Mako: The next SWE Interview Platform 


## The Problem 

SWE interviews have been using the same method for over a decade. With the advent of AI, we have yet to find the utilization of AI enter this sphere. 

Lets look at the following scenarios:

- I want to practice interviews with mocks. I want to know what I did good and wrong here. 
- Interviews prep is largely focused around leetcode, something which is currently going through changes currently as a result of ai. More emphasis on less question based interviews, and more on raw concepts
- Interview preps seem to be lacking in ways to practice system design

Here is where we come in


## The Solution

Mako is the new interview platform. We integrate AI to improve the technical interview sphere and to have you ready for it. 

What we offer: 

- The ability to schedule mock interviews with others 
- The ability to shedule mock interviews with ai interviewer 
- The ability for interview coaches to create their own page, adding content that would be useful for those that choose to subscribe 

And to elevate us over just the consumer space: 

- The ability for companies to outsource their interviews to us. They can use us and our ai interviewer for their specific problem space (i.e. a backend interview, an interview based on frontend ux)

Currently platforms like codesignal and hackerrank are:
1. focused solely on interviews for their new features 
2. do not provide new ways to allow candidates to study 
3. do not allow a lot of feedback for learning other than pass/fail

We'd provide the new way to do interviews. 


## MVP 

Initially we will build out the mock interview pipeline for system design interviews. We figure this is a good way to allow AI to help us out, as well as providing a space that is not that developed. 

What we will deliver:
- Mock interviews between users
- Pay per credit to allow scheduling for interviews 
- Scheduling and creating interviews 

- Mock interviews with ai feedback
- Stretch: mock interview with ai interviewer

From their we will evaluate and work towards more features
